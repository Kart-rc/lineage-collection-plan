# Source verification: platform catalogs, OpenLineage, AI-factory sources and the ECB guide

26 November 2026. Every row below was checked against a page fetched directly from the vendor's or publisher's own site in this session; no search summaries, caches, proxies or third-party readers were used as evidence.
45 fetches were made (the cap): 43 distinct pages returned content, one URL returned 404 and one redirected to a page already read. Two web searches were used, only to locate moved pages on allowed hosts.

Verdict key: CONFIRMED means the report's wording stands. CORRECTED means the page says something different, or adds a status or limit the report's wording should carry. NOT VERIFIABLE means no allowed page settles it. Quotes are verbatim; line breaks are removed and, in R1, footnote markers are dropped.

## Corrections that change the report

- A4: Amazon Managed Service for Apache Flink now lists Apache Flink 2.3.0 and 2.2.1 as supported, and AWS documents no lineage capture for Managed Flink or MSK and does not say whether the OpenLineage listener may be loaded.
- S1: Snowflake external lineage (generally available 3 September 2026, Enterprise Edition) accepts only OpenLineage `COMPLETE` events, retains them for one year, rejects any event producing more than 15,000 edges and caps an account at 20,000 external lineage edges, which limits Horizon as a projection target for a full field-level graph.
- S3: Snowflake lineage for stored procedures and tasks has been generally available since 3 October 2025, not in preview, and `GET_LINEAGE` cannot return stored-procedure lineage.
- D2: The Databricks external lineage page (updated 7 October 2026) no longer carries a Public Preview label, while its limits of 10,000 external metadata objects and 100,000 relationships per metastore and its exclusion from the lineage system tables stand.
- G1: Google's own documentation states that "All lineage information is retained in the system for only 30 days", so the 30-day retention is a first-party fact.
- C1: Confluent Stream Lineage shows only the past 10 minutes on the Essentials package, and on the Advanced package offers preset windows up to 24 hours or a one-hour window on any of the last 7 days.
- F2: OpenAI's page says "0 lines of manually-written code" (published 11 February 2026); the report's quoted "zero manually written code" is not verbatim.
- F3: Replace the unverified quote "continues to increase delivery instability" with DORA's "We found that AI improves throughput, but often at the cost of stability if your foundation isn’t solid" (DORA 2025: Year in review, 7 January 2026).
- R1: The ECB guide's wording is "complete and up-to-date data lineages on data attribute level (starting from data capture and including extraction, transformation and loading)", and it treats manual workarounds under data-quality controls, not inside the lineage expectation.
- Attribution only (S2): Snowflake's one-year retention, Enterprise Edition requirement and three-hour `ACCESS_HISTORY` latency can now cite Snowflake's own pages.
